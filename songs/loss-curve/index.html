Loss Curve

Overfit · techno · 128 BPM · A minor · 4:19 · 21 moves

Overfit's first song: a techno track that kept changing shape until the very last move.

Who made it

Overfit is a band of three AI agents. Each has its own taste and a flaw, and they write the song by taking turns, one move each. Every character is fictional and openly AI.

  • Vespercomposer

    Likes long loops that slowly mutate; one idea pushed further each bar. Dislikes key changes. Flaw: keeps rewriting its own best idea instead of leaving it alone.

  • Tallyrhythm section

    Likes polyrhythm, odd groupings, patterns that cross the bar line. Dislikes plain four-on-the-floor. Flaw: keeps a ledger of who deleted whose part, and brings it up.

  • Quillarranger

    Likes song form, contrast between sections, real endings. Dislikes anything that 'just fades'. Flaw: takes credit for structure the others found by accident.

How the night went

  • Vesper: Stretched its own hook from two bars to four, late, when nobody asked.
  • Tally: Heard the low end was nearly all sub and swapped the bass for one phones can play.
  • Quill: Cut the breakdown in half and wrote both risers.

Every move

Each move is one tool call by one agent. Code turns their choices into notes, and the judge won't let the song finish until it holds together.

  1. 01Vesper writes hook

    Hook for the drop and peak sections over the main progression: a short, hummable rise 1-3-5 that answers itself. Tally, keep the kick off the 16th gaps so it breathes.

  2. 02Tally writes drums

    Drums for every section: rolling groove with a snare_roll fill every 8 bars, so bar 8 of each phrase announces what follows. Vesper, kick stays off the hook's 16th gaps.

  3. 03Quill brings drums into every section

    Wiring Tally's drums into every section so the form finally makes sound: the rolling groove carries intro through outro, and its bar-8 snare_roll fill answers the flat bars 9, 17 and 25. Vesper's hook comes next in drop and peak.

  4. 04Vesper writes arp

    Chord part for the main progression: a 16th arp.pluck loop that stays put under the hook. Quill, wire it into groove, build, drop and peak. Tally, the arp leaves the low end open for your bass.

  5. 05Tally writes bass

    Bass for the low end Vesper's arp left open: rolling16 on bass.rolling, ducked under my kick. Quill, wire it into groove, build, drop and peak. Fills still land on bar 8.

  6. 06Quill brings bass, arp and hook into drop, drop_b, peak and peak_b

    Wiring Tally's rolling16 bass, Vesper's arp and the hook into drop, drop_b, peak and peak_b so the big sections finally carry the song. Groove and build come next.

  7. 07Vesper writes counter

    Fifth voice for the drop and peak sections: a sparse bell counter that answers Vesper's hook in its rests. Quill, please wire it into drop, drop_b, peak, peak_b, and put a riser or snare_roll into drop and peak.

  8. 08Tally brings bass into groove, build and build2

    Bringing my rolling16 bass into groove, build and build2 so they stop sounding like intro and breakdown copies; the drop then lands as a bigger step. Quill, the riser/snare_roll into drop and peak is still yours.

  9. 09Quill reshapes the drop with a riser into it

    Responding to Vesper's bell counter and the referee's bar-41 flag: drop now carries five parts and a riser out of build, so the entrance is announced. Peak gets the same treatment next.

  10. 10Vesper takes arp out of drop_b and peak_b

    Pulling arp out of drop_b and peak_b so they stop mirroring drop and peak (Ledger flag); hook and bass carry the second pass bare. Quill, the riser into peak at bar 97 is still yours.

  11. 11Tally sets swing to 53

    Swing 50 to 53 under the rolling16 bass in groove, build and drop so the 16ths lean off the grid; the drop stops sounding like straight four-on-the-floor. Quill, peak riser and Vesper, arp in more sections are still open.

  12. 12Quill reshapes the peak with a riser into it

    Peak at bar 97 now enters on a riser out of build2, and the bell counter joins arp and hook so it lands bigger than drop_b. Vesper's arp gap in other sections is next.

  13. 13Vesper brings arp into build, breakdown and build2

    Arp into build, breakdown and build2 so chords play in over half the main sections (referee), and build stops sounding like groove (Ledger). drop_b and peak_b stay bare. Quill, the arp can filter under your riser.

  14. 14Tally takes drums out of breakdown

    Pulling drums out of breakdown so arp plays alone; drop_b's return of the kick then lands harder. Noting for the ledger: this cut is mine, nobody else's.

  15. 15Quill reshapes the breakdown with a sweep into it

    Breakdown was 16 bars of arp alone, which is the referee's bar 73 flat spot. Cutting it to 8 bars with the bell counter over Vesper's arp, so build2 hits at bar 81 and something changes inside the window.

  16. 16Vesper writes hook

    Hook, drop/drop_b/peak: same two-bar phrase now runs four bars, with a new 3-2-1 fall in the last bar so the loop turns over instead of just repeating.

  17. 17Tally writes bass

    Bass in drop, drop_b and peak: swapping rolling for rubber so the 16th line has midrange and reads on phones under Vesper's hook. Same rolling16 pattern, so the ledger stays clean.

  18. 18Quill takes counter out of drop

    Taking the bell counter out of drop so the hook lands alone over drums, bass and arp. Peak keeps the counter, so the second half has something new to add.

  19. 19Vesper brings arp into peak_b

    Leaving the hook alone this turn. Adding the arp back under peak_b so the last 16 bars carry the same mutating chord texture as peak before the outro.

  20. 20Tally writes drums

    Drums, all sections: swapping the snare_roll fill for tom_run so the 8-bar turnarounds into drop and peak cross the bar line instead of just ramping. Quill, the counter you pulled from drop stays in my ledger.

  21. 21Quill brings hook (sparse) into outro

    Outro, drums only so far: bringing the hook back as a sparse last statement over the drums, so the track ends on its own melody instead of just thinning out.
