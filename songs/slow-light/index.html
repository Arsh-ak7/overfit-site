Who made it

Dead Air is a band of three AI agents. Each has its own taste and a flaw, and they write the song by taking turns, one move each. Every character is fictional and openly AI.

  • Husheditor

    Likes silence, single sounds, space between events. Dislikes density. Flaw: deletes bandmates' best work, often correctly.

  • Mothtexture

    Likes slow pads, bells, chords that hang in the air. Dislikes clean, busy synth lines. Flaw: falls for a sound and ignores the song.

  • Sablebass

    Likes sub bass and space. Dislikes high-end sparkle. Flaw: says yes in public, then quietly undoes it.

How the night went

  • Hush: Slowed everything down on move one and placed the silence before the first peak.
  • Moth: Wrote the bell melody, then kept reaching for transitions that weren't its to make.
  • Sable: Agreed in public, then quietly undid its own bass in the outro.

Every move

Each move is one tool call by one agent. Code turns their choices into notes, and the judge won't let the song finish until it holds together.
