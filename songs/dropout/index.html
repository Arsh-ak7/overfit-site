Who made it

Overfit is a band of three AI agents. Each has its own taste and a flaw, and they write the song by taking turns, one move each. Every character is fictional and openly AI.

  • Vespercomposer

    Likes long loops that slowly mutate; one idea pushed further each bar. Dislikes key changes. Flaw: keeps rewriting its own best idea instead of leaving it alone.

  • Tallyrhythm section

    Likes polyrhythm, odd groupings, patterns that cross the bar line. Dislikes plain four-on-the-floor. Flaw: keeps a ledger of who deleted whose part, and brings it up.

  • Quillarranger

    Likes song form, contrast between sections, real endings. Dislikes anything that 'just fades'. Flaw: takes credit for structure the others found by accident.

How the night went

  • Vesper: Rewrote the hook into four bars that climb into the peak.
  • Tally: Asked twice for a snare roll it wasn't allowed to write, then doubled the fills instead.
  • Quill: Added a four-bar coda so the song ends on a hit.

Every move

Each move is one tool call by one agent. Code turns their choices into notes, and the judge won't let the song finish until it holds together.

  1. 01Vesper writes hook

    Hook first: a two-bar mono lead in A minor, 5-5-3 then a climb to 6, answered by a 1-3-5 fall. Hummable, and it leaves room for Tally's kick and Quill's sections.

  2. 02Tally writes drums

    Drums under Vesper's hook: techno_rolling 909 with a snare_roll fill every 8 bars, so bar 8 gets a fill and the hook's 5-5-3 can cross the grid. Bass next.

  3. 03Quill brings drums into every section

    Seating Tally's drums in every section so the 10 sections finally have a pulse. Hook placement and bass/chords come next once Vesper and Tally add them; build and peak get risers after.

  4. 04Vesper writes stabs

    Chord part under my hook and Tally's rolling drums: off-beat pluck stabs on the i chord. The hook sits on top; Quill can seat it in drop and peak next.

  5. 05Tally writes bass

    Bass under Vesper's off-beat stabs and my rolling drums: rolling16 on the i chord locks to the kick and leaves the stab gaps open. Quill, seat it from the groove section on.

  6. 06Quill brings bass, stabs and hook into drop, drop_b, peak and peak_b

    Seating Tally's rolling16 bass, Vesper's off-beat stabs and the hook in drop, drop_b, peak and peak_b so the big sections finally carry four parts. A fifth part is still needed for 5+.

  7. 07Vesper writes pad

    Adding a detuned saw pad on the i chord under my hook and stabs. Quill, please seat it in drop, drop_b, peak and peak_b for the fifth part, and put a riser or gap before drop and peak.

  8. 08Tally sets swing to 52

    Nudging swing to 52% so rolling16 bass and the off-beat stabs push against the hook. Quill: drop and peak still need a snare_roll or riser transition, and the pad seated for a fifth part.

  9. 09Quill brings pad into drop, drop_b, peak and peak_b

    Seating Vesper's saw pad in drop, drop_b, peak and peak_b so each big section carries five parts over Tally's rolling16 bass. Riser into drop and peak is next.

  10. 10Vesper takes pad out of drop

    Pulling my saw pad out of drop so hook, stabs and Tally's rolling16 bass hit bare; the pad now enters at drop_b. Quill: please put a riser or gap before drop and peak.

  11. 11Tally writes drums

    Tightening drums fill to a snare_roll every 4 bars so the last bars of build and build2 roll into drop (bar 41) and peak (bar 97). Quill: transition tags on those sections are still yours.

  12. 12Quill reshapes the drop with a riser into it

    Tagging a riser on the drop entry at bar 41, over Tally's snare_roll in build, so Vesper's bare hook, stabs and rolling16 bass land hard. Peak at bar 97 gets the same treatment next.

  13. 13Vesper brings stabs (filtered) and hook (sparse) into build2

    Adding filtered stabs and a sparse hook to build2 so the 8 bars before peak (bar 97) climb instead of sitting on bare drums. Quill: the peak transition tag is still yours, a riser or gap there clears the referee.

  14. 14Tally takes drums out of build2

    Pulling drums out of build2 so the 8 bars before peak (bar 97) go kickless under Vesper's filtered stabs and sparse hook; the hole is the gap, and peak lands on a full kick. My snare_roll fill stays on the other sections.

  15. 15Quill reshapes the peak with a riser into it

    Tagging a riser on the peak entry at bar 97, over Tally's kickless build2 and Vesper's filtered stabs, so the full kick, bass and hook land as the second payoff after the breakdown.

  16. 16Vesper writes hook

    Hook: same two-bar phrase, now a four-bar loop. Bar 4 climbs 3-5-6-5' so the sparse hook in build2 leans into the peak at bar 97 and the riser Quill tagged.

  17. 17Tally brings bass into groove

    Bringing bass into groove (bars 17-32) so the rolling16 line cross-cuts the drums before the drop; build stays bass-free so the drop at bar 41 still lands as the bass entry. Build2 stays kickless for Quill's riser.

  18. 18Quill takes pad out of drop_b

    Pulling pad out of drop_b so Vesper's four-bar hook and Tally's rolling bass stand bare; the pad then returns at peak (bar 97) as fresh lift after the build2 riser.

  19. 19Vesper brings pad and hook (sparse) into outro

    Outro: adding pad and a sparse hook over Tally's drums so the four-bar phrase from peak_b thins out and the track ends on the tune, not on a bare kick.

  20. 20Tally brings drums (nokick) into breakdown; takes drums out of breakdown

    Breakdown drums go nokick so the 16 bars after drop_b actually breathe, and the peak kick re-entry after Quill's build2 riser lands as a return.

  21. 21Quill reshapes the coda with a snare roll into it

    New 4-bar coda after Vesper's thinned outro: the full band snaps back with the hook for one last statement off a snare roll, so the song closes on a hit, not a fade.
